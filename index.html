How to use it

Use it after shampooing, whenever brass shows up or your copper starts to fade.

  1. 1

    Shampoo, then squeeze

    Wash your hair as usual and rinse. Squeeze a generous amount of mask into your palm.

  2. 2

    Apply and wait

    Work it through damp hair from roots to ends. Leave it on for 5 to 10 minutes. Icy Cool Blonde can stay up to 15.

  3. 3

    Rinse

    Rinse well. The longer you leave it on, the more intense the tone.

First time? Very light, platinum and gray hair takes pigment fast. Start with 5 minutes and add time on your next wash if you want a stronger result.

Questions

Which shade is right for me?

Blonde Pearl for highlighted or bleached blondes that turn yellow or brassy and want a warm ivory-pearl blonde. Icy Cool Blonde for very light blonde, platinum and yellowed gray hair. Silver Ash Blonde for highlights that turn orange and should look cool and ashy. Irish Copper for red and copper hair that has faded.

Will it turn my hair purple or blue?

The pigments are strong. Very light, porous or gray hair can pick up a cool tint if the mask stays on too long. Start with 5 minutes, and if you see a tint, it usually softens with your next wash.

Can I use it on gray hair?

Yes. Icy Cool Blonde is made for gray and white hair that has turned yellow. Shoppers also use Silver Ash Blonde on highlights while their gray grows in.

How long do I leave it on?

5 to 10 minutes on damp hair after shampooing. Icy Cool Blonde can stay up to 15 minutes. Longer gives a more intense tone.

Is Irish Copper a toner?

No. Irish Copper is a color-depositing mask for red, copper, auburn and mahogany hair. It revives faded copper tones and is not meant for blonde hair.

What is in it?

Ultra high-purity pigments, argan oil, biotin and pro-vitamin B5. The formula is paraben-free and salt-free.
